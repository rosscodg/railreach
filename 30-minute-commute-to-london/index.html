Frequently asked questions

How many places are within a 30 minute commute to London?

148 stations reach a London terminal in 30 minutes or less on a weekday, across 10 of the 11 main line terminals. 144 of them are direct, with no change of train.

Which is the fastest?

Battersea Park, at 4 minutes into Victoria.

Are these times realistic?

They are the quickest scheduled weekday journey, so a best case rather than a typical morning. Each station page shows the typical peak time alongside, which is the more useful number when judging a daily commute.
