Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest train to London London Bridge?

The fastest connection is from New Cross, at 5 minutes on a direct service. Services into London Bridge are operated by Southeastern, Southern, Thameslink.

Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of London Bridge?

25 stations reach London Bridge in under 30 minutes, including New Cross, Lewisham, Hither Green, Blackheath, East Croydon, Grove Park. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

How many stations connect to London Bridge?

47 stations have a service to London London Bridge within 90 minutes, and 46 of those are direct trains with no change required.

What areas does London Bridge serve?

London London Bridge primarily serves South East London, Kent and Surrey. Key commuter destinations on this network include New Cross, Lewisham, Hither Green, Blackheath, East Croydon.

Is London Bridge a good terminal to commute into?

With 47 stations inside 90 minutes and 46 direct services, London Bridge offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is New Cross at 5 minutes.
