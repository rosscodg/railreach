## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fastest train to London Waterloo?

The fastest connection is from Vauxhall, at 4 minutes on a direct service. Services into Waterloo are operated by South Western Railway.

### Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of Waterloo?

25 stations reach Waterloo in under 30 minutes, including Vauxhall, Clapham Junction, Earlsfield, Putney, Surbiton, Wimbledon. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

### How many stations connect to Waterloo?

126 stations have a service to London Waterloo within 90 minutes, and 106 of those are direct trains with no change required.

### What areas does Waterloo serve?

London Waterloo primarily serves Surrey, Hampshire and the South West. Key commuter destinations on this network include Vauxhall, Clapham Junction, Earlsfield, Putney, Surbiton.

### Is Waterloo a good terminal to commute into?

With 126 stations inside 90 minutes and 106 direct services, Waterloo offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is Vauxhall at 4 minutes.
