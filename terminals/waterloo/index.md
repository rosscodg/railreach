## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fastest train to London Waterloo?

The fastest connection is from Vauxhall, at 4 minutes on a direct service. Services into Waterloo are operated by South Western Railway.

### Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of Waterloo?

24 stations reach Waterloo in under 30 minutes, including Vauxhall, Clapham Junction, Earlsfield, Putney, Surbiton, Wimbledon. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

### How many stations connect to Waterloo?

79 stations have a service to London Waterloo within 90 minutes, and 76 of those are direct trains with no change required.

### What areas does Waterloo serve?

London Waterloo primarily serves Surrey, Hampshire and the South West. Key commuter destinations on this network include Vauxhall, Clapham Junction, Earlsfield, Putney, Surbiton.

### Is Waterloo a good terminal to commute into?

With 79 stations inside 90 minutes and 76 direct services, Waterloo offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is Vauxhall at 4 minutes.

## About this data

Fastest is the quickest scheduled weekday service; typical peak is the median of
services arriving at the London terminal between 07:00 and 09:30. Computed from
Darwin Timetable Files published by the Rail Delivery Group under the Open
Government Licence. Not live times, and they exclude disruption and engineering
work. Full methodology: https://railreach.co.uk/about/
