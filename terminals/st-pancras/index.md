## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fastest train to London St Pancras?

The fastest connection is from Kentish Town, at 5 minutes on a direct service. Services into St Pancras are operated by Thameslink, East Midlands Railway, Southeastern high speed.

### Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of St Pancras?

21 stations reach St Pancras in under 30 minutes, including Kentish Town, Finsbury Park, West Hampstead Thameslink, Cricklewood, Oakleigh Park, Mill Hill Broadway. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

### How many stations connect to St Pancras?

84 stations have a service to London St Pancras within 90 minutes, and 84 of those are direct trains with no change required.

### What areas does St Pancras serve?

London St Pancras primarily serves Bedfordshire, the Midlands and, via HS1, the Medway towns. Key commuter destinations on this network include Kentish Town, Finsbury Park, West Hampstead Thameslink, Cricklewood, Oakleigh Park.

### Is St Pancras a good terminal to commute into?

With 84 stations inside 90 minutes and 84 direct services, St Pancras offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is Kentish Town at 5 minutes.
