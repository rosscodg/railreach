Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest train to London Liverpool Street?

The fastest connection is from Stratford, at 7 minutes on a direct service. Services into Liverpool Street are operated by Greater Anglia.

Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of Liverpool Street?

7 stations reach Liverpool Street in under 30 minutes, including Stratford, Tottenham Hale, Romford, Cheshunt, Shenfield, Brentwood. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

How many stations connect to Liverpool Street?

34 stations have a service to London Liverpool Street within 90 minutes, and 33 of those are direct trains with no change required.

What areas does Liverpool Street serve?

London Liverpool Street primarily serves Essex, Hertfordshire and East Anglia. Key commuter destinations on this network include Stratford, Tottenham Hale, Romford, Cheshunt, Shenfield.

Is Liverpool Street a good terminal to commute into?

With 34 stations inside 90 minutes and 33 direct services, Liverpool Street serves a focused commuter corridor. The quickest option is Stratford at 7 minutes.
