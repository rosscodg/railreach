## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fastest train to London Liverpool Street?

The fastest connection is from Stratford, at 6 minutes on a direct service. Services into Liverpool Street are operated by Greater Anglia, Elizabeth line.

### Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of Liverpool Street?

16 stations reach Liverpool Street in under 30 minutes, including Stratford, Tottenham Hale, Abbey Wood, Acton Main Line, Romford, Ealing Broadway. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

### How many stations connect to Liverpool Street?

105 stations have a service to London Liverpool Street within 90 minutes, and 82 of those are direct trains with no change required.

### What areas does Liverpool Street serve?

London Liverpool Street primarily serves Essex, Hertfordshire and East Anglia. Key commuter destinations on this network include Stratford, Tottenham Hale, Abbey Wood, Acton Main Line, Romford.

### Is Liverpool Street a good terminal to commute into?

With 105 stations inside 90 minutes and 82 direct services, Liverpool Street offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is Stratford at 6 minutes.
