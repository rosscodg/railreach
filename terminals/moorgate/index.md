## Frequently asked questions

### What is the fastest train to London Moorgate?

The fastest connection is from Finsbury Park, at 14 minutes on a direct service. Services into Moorgate are operated by Great Northern.

### Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of Moorgate?

6 stations reach Moorgate in under 30 minutes, including Finsbury Park, Alexandra Palace, Hornsey, Oakleigh Park, New Barnet, Enfield Chase. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

### How many stations connect to Moorgate?

18 stations have a service to London Moorgate within 90 minutes, and 18 of those are direct trains with no change required.

### What areas does Moorgate serve?

London Moorgate primarily serves the City, and the Hertford loop and Welwyn inner suburbs. Key commuter destinations on this network include Finsbury Park, Alexandra Palace, Hornsey, Oakleigh Park, New Barnet.

### Is Moorgate a good terminal to commute into?

With 18 stations inside 90 minutes and 18 direct services, Moorgate serves a focused commuter corridor. The quickest option is Finsbury Park at 14 minutes.
