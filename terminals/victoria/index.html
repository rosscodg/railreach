Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest train to London Victoria?

The fastest connection is from Battersea Park, at 4 minutes on a direct service. Services into Victoria are operated by Southeastern, Southern.

Which commuter towns are within 30 minutes of Victoria?

14 stations reach Victoria in under 30 minutes, including Battersea Park, Clapham Junction, Wandsworth Common, Balham, Denmark Hill, Peckham Rye. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

How many stations connect to Victoria?

50 stations have a service to London Victoria within 90 minutes, and 50 of those are direct trains with no change required.

What areas does Victoria serve?

London Victoria primarily serves Kent, Sussex and the South Coast. Key commuter destinations on this network include Battersea Park, Clapham Junction, Wandsworth Common, Balham, Denmark Hill.

Is Victoria a good terminal to commute into?

With 50 stations inside 90 minutes and 50 direct services, Victoria offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is Battersea Park at 4 minutes.
