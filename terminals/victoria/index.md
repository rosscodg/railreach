The fastest connection is from Battersea Park, at 4 minutes on a direct service. Services into Victoria are operated by Southeastern, Southern.

17 stations reach Victoria in under 30 minutes, including Battersea Park, Clapham Junction, Denmark Hill, Balham, Wandsworth Common, Peckham Rye. These are the shortest commutes available into this terminal.

### How many stations connect to Victoria?

67 stations have a service to London Victoria within 90 minutes, and 67 of those are direct trains with no change required.

### What areas does Victoria serve?

London Victoria primarily serves Kent, Sussex and the South Coast. Key commuter destinations on this network include Battersea Park, Clapham Junction, Denmark Hill, Balham, Wandsworth Common.

### Is Victoria a good terminal to commute into?

With 67 stations inside 90 minutes and 67 direct services, Victoria offers unusually broad commuter coverage. The quickest option is Battersea Park at 4 minutes.
