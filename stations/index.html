Frequently asked questions

Which commuter towns are closest to London by train?

Of the towns with a full RailReach guide, 11 are within 30 minutes of a London terminal: Slough, Watford Junction, Bromley South, Richmond, Maidenhead, St Albans City, Stevenage, Reading, Woking, Sevenoaks, High Wycombe. The quickest is Slough at 15 minutes into Paddington.

How do I compare two commuter towns?

Each station page lists every London terminal that town can reach, the journey time, whether the train is direct, and the operator — so two towns can be compared on identical measures.

Does RailReach cover every UK station?

The dataset covers 345 stations that reach a London terminal within 90 minutes. Detailed guides currently exist for 27 of the most searched commuter towns, and the interactive map covers all 345.
