Frequently asked questions

Is there a direct train from Henley-on-Thames to London?

No. Across three midweek days of timetable data there is no direct service from Henley-on-Thames to any London terminal RailReach covers. Reaching London means changing, usually at the nearest junction on the main line.

Which London terminal does Henley-on-Thames connect towards?

Services from Henley-on-Thames feed towards London Paddington, operated by Great Western Railway, Elizabeth line. The connecting journey time depends on the change and is not published here, because it has not been measured.

Why does RailReach not give a journey time for Henley-on-Thames?

Every time on this site is computed from published timetables. A journey involving a change requires routing across services, which this dataset does not do, so no figure is given rather than an estimated one.
