Frequently asked questions

Is there a direct train from Littlehampton to London?

No. Across three midweek days of timetable data there is no direct service from Littlehampton to any London terminal RailReach covers. Reaching London means changing, usually at the nearest junction on the main line.

Which London terminal does Littlehampton connect towards?

Services from Littlehampton feed towards London Victoria, operated by Southeastern, Southern. The connecting journey time depends on the change and is not published here, because it has not been measured.

Why does RailReach not give a journey time for Littlehampton?

Every time on this site is computed from published timetables. A journey involving a change requires routing across services, which this dataset does not do, so no figure is given rather than an estimated one.
